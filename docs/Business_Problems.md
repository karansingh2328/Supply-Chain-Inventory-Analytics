# Business Problems

The company may be facing the following business problems:

- Why is the company highly dependent on discounts to generate sales?
- Why are sales declining in some countries and markets?
- Why are only a few product categories contributing the majority of total sales?
- Why are some products generating low or negative profit?
- Why do some products require significantly higher discounts than others?
- Why are delivery delays occurring frequently?
- How are delivery delays affecting business performance?
- Which markets and countries are consistently underperforming in sales and profitability?
- Why are some product categories consistently underperforming?
- Why are some customer segments generating lower sales than others?
- Why is overall profitability under pressure despite strong sales performance?
- Which areas of the supply chain require operational improvement?
