## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, shipping performance, and supply chain efficiency to identify business opportunities and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used

- **Python** — Data Cleaning, Feature Engineering, and Exploratory Data Analysis
- **Microsoft Excel** — Business Analysis
- **PostgreSQL** — SQL Business Analysis
- **Power BI** — Dashboard Development and Visualization

## Project Scope

This project focuses on analyzing:

- Sales Performance
- Profitability
- Customer Behavior
- Product Performance
- Shipping Performance
- Delivery Performance
- Discount Impact
- Supply Chain Efficiency

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs were analyzed throughout the project:

- **Total Sales:** $36,784,735.01
- **Total Profit:** $3,966,902.97
- **Total Orders:** 65,752
- **Total Customers:** 20,652
- **Average Profit Margin:** Calculated in Power BI
- **Average Shipping Time:** Calculated in Power BI
- **Late Delivery Rate:** Calculated in Power BI
- **Average Discount Rate:** Calculated in Power BI

## Business Objectives

This project aims to:

- Identify sales and profit trends.
- Identify underperforming products and categories.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on sales and profitability.
- Analyze shipping and delivery performance.
- Identify underperforming markets and countries.
- Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to support business decisions.
