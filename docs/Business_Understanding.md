# Business Understanding

## Business Type

The company operates in the retail supply chain industry. It sells products across multiple countries and manages customer orders, shipping, and product deliveries throughout the supply chain.

## Product Categories

The company sells products across multiple product categories, including electronics, computers, sports, fishing, fitness, apparel, and other retail product categories.

## Customer Segments

The company serves three major customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

## Geographic Presence

The company operates across multiple geographical locations, including:

- **164 Countries**
- **5 Markets**
- **3,597 Cities**

This wide geographical presence enables the business to serve customers across multiple international markets.

## Revenue Source

The company generates revenue through product sales across multiple geographical locations.

Revenue performance is influenced by factors such as:

- Product demand
- Order quantity
- Discounts
- Product categories
- Customer segments
- Shipping performance
- Market and country performance

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, shipping performance, and supply chain efficiency to identify business problems, evaluate operational performance, uncover improvement opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.
